This is the U24 re-run (2026-07-25) of Linux-VitBox, not a replacement for it.

Why it exists: the original Linux-VitBox VM ran a Debian 13 guest whose
userspace (7-Zip, GCC, OpenSSL) differed in version from the Ubuntu 24.04
bare-metal baseline, confounding a handful of tests (7zip, openssl, stress_ng,
kernel_compile, fractal — see PROJECT_NOTES.md "Known confound — userspace islands").
This folder re-runs just those tests plus sysbench_cpu (version-invariant
control) on an Ubuntu 24.04 guest instead, so the guest-image confound is
removed for this subset.

Only 9 tests here (not the full 23) — fio/network/memory were version-clean
already and were not re-run; see Linux-VitBox/ for those numbers, which still
stand. Collected with bench.py, not benchmark.sh — see PROJECT_NOTES.md
"bench.py v6.0-reduced" for the full rationale and the sysbench-control result
(the ~11% CPU overhead in Linux-VitBox turned out to be the guest image, not
the hypervisor).

Kept side by side with Linux-VitBox/ on purpose: the pair is itself the
measurement of how much of the original result was the guest image.
