This is the v7 topology re-run (2026-07-26) of Win-HyperV, not a replacement.

Why it exists: this was the worst-confounded platform in v5 — the Hyper-V
guest was handed 2 physical cores + 2 SMT threads each (4 vCPUs total, but
only 2 real cores), the most distorted topology of any platform in the
Windows round. Fixed the same way as WSL2/Docker: host BIOS Hyper-Threading
disabled (host now 4c/4t), guest VM recreated with
Set-VMProcessor -HwThreadCountPerCore 1 so it now genuinely gets 4 distinct
physical cores.

Result: sysbench CPU overhead vs the Win-WSL2-v7 baseline came out at -0.28%
(multi) / -0.29% (single) — essentially zero, both within measurement noise
(CV 0.04-0.12%). This is the headline finding of the whole v7 round: Hyper-V's
real CPU cost relative to WSL2 is negligible once the topology confound is
removed. Full 10 iterations, all RUN_COMPLETE ok=10 fail=0. Platform identity
is "Hyper-V-v7" in meta.env, kept distinct from v5's confounded "Hyper-V" rows.

See PROJECT_NOTES.md and WINDOWS_RERUN.md for the full v7 round plan.
